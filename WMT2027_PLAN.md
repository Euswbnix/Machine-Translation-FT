# WMT 2027 研究计划

> 由三路并行分析生成:WMT 接收论文实证调研 / 对抗式预审 / 严谨实验协议设计。

## 论文命题

Top-k quality-estimation filtering is not a pure quality operation but a covert domain-selection operation, and the sign and magnitude of its downstream translation-quality effect can be predicted before any training from a single divergence statistic between the filtered corpus and the evaluation distribution - which reconciles published reports that QE filtering helps with our finding that it hurts as two points on one dose-response curve.

## 推荐方向

HYBRID "A-prime": a measurement/diagnostic paper on QE data filtering, using the existing from-scratch Transformers as the INSTRUMENT and a small-LLM SFT arm as the relevance bridge. Working title: "Filtering for Quality Selects for Domain: Predicting the Downstream Effect of QE-Based Data Selection Before Training." Concretely: (1) build an orthogonalizing resampling construction over the existing 30M-pair CometKiwi-scored en-fr corpus that makes translation-correctness (QE score) and domain composition independently manipulable; (2) show that the standard global top-k-by-QE operation moves BOTH axes and attribute the downstream metric delta between them; (3) define a pre-training-time divergence statistic (Moore-Lewis-style mean cross-entropy-difference shift of the filtered set relative to the evaluation distribution) and fit a dose-response curve, with seed-level confidence intervals, that PREDICTS the sign and magnitude of the downstream effect on held-out filter configurations, a second QE filter model, a second language pair, and a small open LLM fine-tuned on the same conditions; (4) report the seed-noise floor for filtering comparisons, which is what the multi-seed asset is actually for. Base-vs-Big is amputated to a one-paragraph pipeline-calibration note in the appendix with no claim attached; the corrected token budget appears as a fixed instrument, not as a result.

## 为什么是它

Three constraints jointly force this answer. (1) VENUE: 33% of accepted WMT research papers 2023-2025 are evaluation/measurement/meta-evaluation, and 25% are analysis-only with no new method; the highest-taste template at the venue (Shayegh et al., 2025.wmt-1.16, adequacy-fluency) is exactly "name two axes, show the standard instrument is biased toward one, attribute the bias to a concrete cause, propose a control." A measurement claim about how to attribute a filtering-induced quality change is that genre; "Big is not better than Base" is not. (2) ORIGINALITY: every capacity, variance, and taxonomy claim in the old paper is pre-empted (Vaswani 2017/Popel-Bojar 2018 for capacity; Clark 2011, Reimers-Gurevych 2017, Dodge 2020, Mosbach 2021, Sellam 2022, Jordan 2023, Zhou-Savova 2025 for variance; WMT18 filtering task, Moore-Lewis 2010, Axelrod 2011/2017 for the correctness-vs-domain taxonomy; Peter et al. 2023 and Wang et al. 2020 for QE filtering and multi-domain curricula). What is NOT pre-empted is a pre-training-time predictor of the effect, fitted and validated out of sample. That is a genuinely new object, not a restatement. (3) ASSETS AND SOLO CAPACITY: the 30M-pair CometKiwi-scored corpus, the provenance segmentation, the from-scratch pipeline, the 24 checkpoints, the ~0.5h FT loop, and the statistical craft all survive into this plan and are the majority of the work; direction B discards three of the four and puts a solo researcher in a race with Unbabel/Tower/Google that resolves faster than one submission cycle; direction C discards all four and is off-scope for the venue. The recentering also CUTS compute versus the naive repair: killing the 3-size x 3-scale x 10-seed capacity grid drops the budget from ~2,180 H200-hours to ~600-800, which buys back the months the presentation failures (acl.sty font, missing hyperparameter appendix, 219-word related work, unreadable Figure 1) actually need. Finally, the base rate is 39% acceptance from ~55 submissions/yr, not 32% from 60-85: with R2 already at 4 on precisely the axis this plan promotes to headline, converting two reviewers on a soundness-and-originality repair is a rational bet, and this design converts BOTH complaints into the paper's contribution rather than merely answering them.

## 被否掉的方向(含理由)

- DIRECTION C (mean-field / interacting-particle theory validation) - KILLED OUTRIGHT. Three independent fatalities: (a) venue mismatch, zero theory-validation precedent in 64 accepted WMT papers 2023-2025 and no theory category in the CFP, so relevance scores 1 on scope alone; (b) mathematical non-starter, the theorems are asymptotic in depth-as-time with weight sharing, and a 6+6-layer encoder-decoder with distinct per-layer weights gives six steps of a non-autonomous system in which a two-timescale metastable plateau cannot be observed; (c) the 'nobody checked on trained models' premise is false - the theorists ran their own numerics (arXiv 2305.05465, Bull. AMS 62(3) 2025) and Rigollet's 2025 survey already catalogues what is and is not established, while Dong et al. ICML 2021, Zhai et al. ICML 2023, Godey et al. EACL 2024 and Razzhigaev et al. 2024 have already published the likely punchline. Adjudicating whether a mean-field theorem's hypotheses are satisfied is not transferable from applied statistics and needs a co-author. If the material is compelling, read it for 12 months with no submission attached and revisit in 2028 at ICLR/TMLR - do not spend the WMT 2027 slot on it.

- DIRECTION B AS STATED (pivot to multi-seed variance in LLM-based MT) - KILLED. Zhou & Savova (arXiv 2503.07329, IJCNLP-AACL 2025) already published the headline claim, the ten-seed LoRA design, AND the 'the field does this wrong' audit of 85 ACL papers; substituting MT for GLUE is a dataset swap, not a contribution. 'Single runs are unreliable' is a 2011 MT finding (Clark, Dyer, Lavie & Smith). The likely measured effect is small (SFT from a model that already translates), producing an unpublishable null that also contradicts the previous paper. It would confound five variance sources (training seed, data order, inference nondeterminism, decoding config, metric/test-set noise) unless batch-invariant kernels are installed first, which is a systems project. And it discards the corpus, the QE scores, the provenance labels and the pipeline to compete on statistics alone against labs for whom this is a footnote. NOTE: the one salvageable fragment - quantifying how much of a published effect sits inside seed noise - is retained as contribution 3 below, at zero marginal compute, applied to the filtering literature where nobody has done it.

- DIRECTION A AS STATED (fix the token budget, re-run Base vs Big, keep the capacity story) - KILLED. Matching budgets, tuning Big, and training to convergence makes Big win, which reproduces Vaswani et al. 2017 and Popel & Bojar 2018; the headline becomes 'we fixed our own bug and recovered the 2017 result.' Worse, the variance ratio is confounded at BOTH ends - Big saw 0.23-0.71x Base's tokens while Base ran 43-181 epochs (975 tok/param en-fr full vs Vaswani's ~36-41), and an over-epoched, dropout-0.1, label-smoothed Base converges into a flat low-variance basin - so the 3-4x std ratio is mechanically inflated on both sides and Mosbach et al. ICLR 2021 predicts it collapses once training is corrected. Twelve months to confirm R3's one-line objection.

- SUB-CLAIM KILLED: 'Big shows 3-4x higher seed std' as a headline finding. Pre-empted (Jordan 2023 measured 8x test-set variance for BERT-large over 1,000 fine-tunings; Sellam et al. ICLR 2022 built MultiBERTs around it), and at n=4 the exact 95% CI on the std ratio is [0.79, 12.24] - it does not exclude the null direction, so the previous paper could not support the claim under any test. Even n=10 leaves roughly [1.6, 6.3]. Demoted to a noise-floor appendix used as SUPPORT for the filtering CIs.

- SUB-CLAIM KILLED: the two-axis decomposition of data quality (translation correctness vs domain alignment) as a NOVELTY claim. This is the literal framing of the WMT 2018 Parallel Corpus Filtering shared task, which states it addresses data quality and not domain-relatedness and discourages subsampling for news relevance, and it is the premise of Moore & Lewis 2010, Axelrod/He/Gao 2011, Axelrod 2017, van der Wees et al. 2017, surveyed as settled in Saunders JAIR 2022. R2's Originality=4 was given from outside the corpus-filtering literature. It is demoted to cited background with the WMT18 task quoted; the paper contributes the measurement, not the taxonomy.

- SUB-CLAIM KILLED: domain-conditional QE filtering as a METHOD contribution. Wang et al. ACL 2020 (multi-domain curriculum combining instance-level domain-relevance with quality/noise features) and Junczys-Dowmunt's WMT18 dual conditional cross-entropy system already own this, and the expected result ('it works') is what everyone assumes. It survives only as one CONTROL CONDITION inside the dose-response grid - the point on the curve where induced domain shift is held near zero - never as the paper's proposal.

- SUB-CLAIM KILLED: the capped-vs-full-stream 'corpus regime' axis. 'First 10M of the HuggingFace stream' varies size, domain composition, noise (93% vs 74% keep rate), source ordering and per-pair exposure (~1290 vs ~1960 token-visits/pair) simultaneously; the -1.5 BLEU 'more data hurts' result is uninterpretable and a caveat sentence does not repair it. All corpus subsets in the new plan are uniform random draws from ONE cleaned pool, with domain composition reintroduced as a deliberate, separately manipulated factor.

- KILLED: arXiv preprinting (V1 was desk-rejected on moderation; per the user's own standing note, peer-review path only). Pre-registration and artifact release go to OSF/Zenodo with a DOI and a timestamped git tag instead.

## 原创性论证(针对 Originality=1 的正面回应)

THE NEW CLAIM, stated so it can be checked against prior art: the downstream effect of quality-based data filtering is PREDICTABLE BEFORE TRAINING from a single divergence statistic between the filtered corpus and the evaluation distribution, because top-k QE selection covertly performs domain selection; the predictor is fitted with seed-level uncertainty, validated OUT OF SAMPLE on held-out filter configurations, a second filter model, a second language pair, and a different model paradigm; and it reconciles the published positive result with our negative one as two points on one curve. Four things make this survive Originality=1, each paired with the prior work that does NOT contain it. (1) THE PREDICTOR. Peter et al. 2023 ('There's No Data like Better Data', 2023.wmt-1.50, Google) show QE filtering improves quality at half the data, with an analysis of the filtering results; Finkelstein et al. 2024 (2024.wmt-1.126) show QE/MBR-reranked data beats a 300x larger corpus. Both establish a POSITIVE POINT ESTIMATE in one regime. Neither predicts when the effect reverses, neither computes anything before training, and neither reports a dose-response. My contribution is the curve and the pre-training-time statistic that indexes position on it - which explains their result and mine simultaneously instead of contradicting either. (2) THE ORTHOGONALIZATION. The WMT 2018 Parallel Corpus Filtering task explicitly frames quality separately from domain-relatedness and discourages subsampling for news relevance; Moore & Lewis 2010, Axelrod/He/Gao 2011, Axelrod 2017 and van der Wees et al. 2017 supply cross-entropy-difference domain selection. That literature owns the TAXONOMY and the FORWARD direction (how to select in-domain data). It does not contain the reverse measurement - that a quality-only score, applied exactly as recommended, silently moves the domain axis by a measurable amount, nor a construction that holds one axis fixed while moving the other in a real corpus. I therefore cite Moore-Lewis as my INSTRUMENT, not as my competitor, which removes the most dangerous prior-art collision by absorbing it. (3) NOT A FILTERING METHOD. Wang et al. ACL 2020 (multi-domain curriculum) and Junczys-Dowmunt's WMT18 dual conditional cross-entropy already own combined quality+domain selection, and it demonstrably works. That is exactly why domain-conditional filtering appears here as a CONTROL CONDITION - the near-zero-shift point on the curve - and never as the proposal. The deliverable is a diagnostic a practitioner runs on a candidate filtered corpus before spending GPU hours, not another filter. (4) THE NOISE FLOOR. Clark, Dyer, Lavie & Smith ACL 2011 established optimizer instability for SMT/MERT; Reimers & Gurevych 2017, Dodge et al. 2020, Mosbach et al. 2021, Sellam et al. ICLR 2022, Jordan 2023 and Zhou & Savova 2025 established it for neural fine-tuning and classification. None of them establishes the minimum detectable difference for CORPUS-FILTERING comparisons in NMT, and no accepted WMT research paper 2023-2025 uses multiple seeds at all (zero of 21 in 2025; two of 25 in 2024, only one replicating). So multi-seed rigor is not the finding - it is the reason my dose-response has error bars and the published single-run filtering deltas do not, which is a claim only this author is positioned to make at this venue. WHAT I AM EXPLICITLY NOT CLAIMING, so a reviewer cannot find it and score me down: not that larger models are worse (Vaswani 2017, Popel & Bojar 2018), not that larger models are noisier (Jordan 2023, Sellam et al. 2022), not that data quality decomposes into correctness and domain (WMT18 task, Moore & Lewis 2010), not that domain-conditional filtering works (Wang et al. 2020, Junczys-Dowmunt 2018), and not that single runs are unreliable (Clark et al. 2011).

PHASE 0 / E0.3 - FINE-TUNING CONFOUND CONTROLS, THE GO/NO-GO GATE (~1 GPU-day, 4 conditions x 3 seeds x ~0.5h). The existing QE-FT result has no control and its mechanism is unsupported: optimizer state was reset and a Noam schedule restarted at effective peak 2.7e-4 (Base) / 2.0e-4 (Big) on a converged checkpoint, and BLEU falls monotonically from the FIRST eval - the textbook signature of a too-large restart LR plus catastrophic forgetting, not of domain mismatch. Also note scripts/filter_by_score.py writes the top-K SORTED HIGH-TO-LOW and does not preserve order, so unless the trainer reshuffles, the FT run saw a monotonically degrading quality curriculum - a third candidate explanation for a monotone decline. Run: (a) random-1M control at matched size and identical schedule; (b) bottom-1M-by-QE control; (c) LR sweep spanning >=1 decade including a rate low enough that BLEU is flat; (d) shuffled vs sorted feed order; (e) a held-out UN/legislative AND Europarl test set to demonstrate the in-domain GAIN the domain story predicts (currently only a training-loss curve is reported). PRE-REGISTERED DECISION RULE: proceed to the full program only if, at the flat-LR setting with shuffled order, top-k-QE FT degrades news-domain eval more than the matched random control by more than the seed-noise floor AND improves the UN/legislative eval. This is one GPU-day and it decides whether the observation that motivates the paper is real.

### E4
PHASE 1 / E1.1 - CORRECTED INSTRUMENT (engineering + ~40 H200-h). configs/sft_base_enfr.yaml has 24576 x 4 = 98,304 tok/step and configs/sft_big_enfr.yaml has 8192 x 4 = 32,768 - a memory workaround, not a design choice. Fix by holding the EFFECTIVE non-pad-token batch identical and absorbing the difference into gradient accumulation (Big: micro-batch 8192 x accum 12 = 98,304). HOLD IDENTICAL across models: effective batch in non-pad target tokens, total steps and total tokens, per-seed data order, tokenizer/vocab/max_seq_len/test sets/beam=5/lp/checkpoint-averaging rule, optimizer family and betas/eps/weight-decay/clip/label-smoothing/bf16, LR schedule SHAPE with warmup as a FRACTION of total steps (not 4K vs 8K absolute), and the stopping rule. TUNE PER MODEL, identically: peak LR and dropout only, 12 trials, same space, same selection metric, on a tuning seed excluded from the reporting seeds, pre-registered. Forcing a shared LR across d=512 and d=1024 would be a NEW confound, not a fix.

### E5
PHASE 1 / E1.2 - THE ORTHOGONALIZATION CONSTRUCTION (the methodological contribution; CPU only). From the single cleaned 30M pool with exact provenance and CometKiwi scores, build four condition families, ALL AT IDENTICAL SIZE (5M pairs) so size is never a factor: ARM R = uniform random 5M (baseline). ARM N = naive global top-k by QE at k in {50%, 30%, 15%, 5%} then subsampled to 5M - the standard practice, which moves quality AND domain together. ARM Q = domain-STRATIFIED top-k: select the same quality quantiles WITHIN each provenance bucket at the pool's bucket proportions, so mean QE rises exactly as in Arm N while domain composition is held fixed. ARM D = quality-MATCHED domain resampling: target several domain mixtures (news-heavy, UN-heavy, pool-matched) while matching the QE score distribution decile-by-decile, so domain shifts and quality does not. ARM C = domain-conditional filtering (the config already exists at configs/sft_base_enfr_news.yaml) as the near-zero-shift control point. This is the analogue of the encipherment design in 2025.wmt-1.14: it isolates the confound BY CONSTRUCTION rather than adjusting for it, which is what this venue rewards.

### E10
PHASE 2 / E2.4 - THE DOMAIN-SHIFT STATISTIC AND DOSE-RESPONSE FIT (CPU + ~20h scoring). Primary estimator, label-free and cheap: S = the mean Moore-Lewis cross-entropy difference of the filtered set (in-domain LM on eval-domain monolingual text minus general LM on the pool), i.e. the classical selection score used here as a MEASURING INSTRUMENT rather than as a competing method - which turns Moore & Lewis 2010 from prior art into supporting citation. Also promote the existing ad-hoc 75.1% newstest type-coverage number into a proper statistic with CIs. ## 最大风险

The mechanism may not exist. Two nested failure modes. FIRST, the Phase 0 gate: the -1.5/-2.3 BLEU fine-tuning degradation may be an optimization artifact rather than domain shift - optimizer state was reset and a Noam schedule restarted at 2.7e-4/2.0e-4 on a converged checkpoint with BLEU falling from the very first eval, and scripts/filter_by_score.py writes the top-K sorted high-to-low, so the run may also have seen a monotone quality curriculum. If the matched random-1M control degrades as much as the QE-top subset at a flat LR with shuffled data, the domain explanation dies. SECOND, and more dangerous because it is not detectable in one GPU-day: even with the observation real, Arm Q (quality raised, domain held fixed) may behave identically to Arm N (naive global top-k), which would mean the downstream effect is driven by the quality axis after all and the domain-shift statistic has no predictive coefficient - a flat dose-response and no diagnostic. MITIGATIONS, in order of strength. (a) The gate is deliberately placed in Month 1 and costs one GPU-day, so the expensive commitment is never made on an unvalidated premise. (b) The paper is architected so that two of its three contributions survive a flat result: the orthogonalizing construction is a reusable experimental framework independent of the answer it produces (the precedent, 2025.wmt-1.14, offers its design as the contribution), and the seed-noise floor for filtering comparisons falls out of the same runs at zero marginal compute regardless of sign. (c) The null-handling plan is pre-registered before any Phase 2 run: a well-powered null - 'the intuitive domain-shift explanation for QE-filtering effects is wrong, and here is the well-powered evidence plus the construction that establishes it' - is a real, honest, submittable correction of a mechanism the field currently assumes, and this venue demonstrably accepts nuanced and negative results (25% of accepted 2023-2025 papers introduce no method; Helsinki's 2025.wmt-1.20 was accepted while concluding its own method underperforms a baseline LLM). (d) Pre-registration is what prevents the specific failure this researcher is most exposed to under a flat result - hunting across filter strengths, metrics and test sets for the configuration where the effect appears, which would forfeit the one asset (statistical credibility) that the whole plan is built on. The residual risk that cannot be mitigated is scoop risk on the LLM bridge arm, which is why that arm is scheduled last, kept small, and framed as validation of the small-model predictor rather than as a standalone LLM contribution.
